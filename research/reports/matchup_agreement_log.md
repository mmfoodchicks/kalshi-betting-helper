The owner, 2026-10-23: "I want you and fable to agree on anything built or discovered, I don't
want something to slip through."

**The protocol.** Every deliverable of the matchup-engine upgrade, and every finding it reports,
is reviewed twice, independently:
1. **Fable:** a FRESH instance with no stake in the work. It re-runs the code, re-derives the
   math, and checks leakage, labelling (observed / proxy / modelled), overstated claims and
   silent failures. It writes `research/reviews/<deliverable>.md` with agree / disagree / cannot
   verify on each claim, and findings by severity. A Fable-built deliverable is reviewed by a
   different Fable instance AND the orchestrator.
2. **The orchestrator (Claude),** independently.

An item ships only when both reviews AGREE, or a disagreement is resolved with evidence recorded
here. Unresolved items are blocked and listed as such. Fixes that come out of a review are
re-checked by both before shipping.
