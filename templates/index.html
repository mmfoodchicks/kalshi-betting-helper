How to read this ⓘ
  • Buy YES = you bet the price will hit the target; Buy NO = you bet it won't. Each costs some cents (0–100¢) and pays 100¢ if it wins.
  • Fair value = the model's estimate of what the contract is truly worth. Edge = fair value − the price you'd pay. A bigger positive edge = a better deal.
  • You can sell anytime before close. Tap “I bought this” and enter what you paid — the app will then tell you when to sell in “Tracked markets” below.
